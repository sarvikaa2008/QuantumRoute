How Route Ranking Works

The current prototype compares route estimates using planning time, distance, road-class penalties and possible restrictions found in mapped road tags. Routes with flagged restrictions are heavily penalized. Missing map information does not mean a road is safe.

Research prototype only: This website is not a validated emergency dispatch system.
